# Songlass Code of Conduct

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project community spaces.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the change and its effects, not a contributor's worth or identity.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and give people a chance to clarify or correct mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others or publish another person's private information.
- Respect copyright, licensing and confidentiality. Do not share private project material or credentials.

## AI assistance is allowed and must be disclosed

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Disclose AI assistance used to prepare submitted code, tests, documentation, design work, or PR/issue text. Briefly identify the parts it helped with and how you checked them; naming the tool or model is optional.

The contributor remains responsible for accuracy, licensing, security and explaining the work. Do not submit generated material without reviewing it. Do not post private prompts, conversations or sensitive data to prove disclosure.

Review work on its merits. Shaming someone for using AI, or making unsupported accusations about their process, is not acceptable. The practical disclosure format is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#ai-assistance).

## Reporting a concern

Contact Commander Chara at [@cmdr_chara on X](https://x.com/cmdr_chara) by direct message if that option is available, or use an existing private channel with the maintainer. If you cannot reach a private channel, request one on X or open a minimal GitHub issue **without posting the incident details, names or personal information**. GitHub profiles do not provide a general private-messaging service.

Share only information needed to understand the concern and your preferred way to be contacted. Maintainers should limit access to those handling the report and avoid publishing identifying details without consent, except where disclosure is legally required.

For a software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting exploit details publicly.

## Moderation

Maintainers may request a correction or disclosure, remove harmful material, pause a discussion, or restrict participation. Responses should consider severity, impact and repeated behavior; serious threats or exposure of private information may require immediate restrictions.

People involved should have an opportunity to clarify their account when practical. A concern about a moderation decision can be raised through the same private reporting route. Community moderation does not change rights independently granted by the software licenses.
